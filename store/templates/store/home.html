Maah

by amirtha

Maah is inspired by the first love we know, a mother’s embrace. Derived from “Maah,” meaning mother, the brand represents nurture, warmth, and the earliest lessons of beauty.   The logo integrates the Malayalam mother-tongue letter “മ” (Ma) with the Latin “M,” symbolising heritage and continuity where tradition meets contemporary expression. Its form flows like a gentle drape, reflecting how a mother first teaches us to wrap ourselves in beauty and identity. Soft curves express care and femininity, while the structured “M” adds strength and stability, balancing tenderness with resilience.
